## Highlights

Fan control now tells you when it cannot work instead of pretending it can. If the privileged helper is not installed or has not been approved, ChillMac says so and gives you a button to fix it.

This release re-registers that helper, so macOS will ask you to approve ChillMac once under Login Items & Extensions. Fan control stays unavailable until you do. Later releases go back to a single click.

## Fixed

- **The window could open completely blank.** The popover sometimes showed only its background with every card invisible, reported on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs
- **The menu bar icon closes the window again.** Clicking it while the window was open closed and immediately reopened it, so it never appeared to close at all
- **Fan control did nothing on Intel Macs from 2018 onward.** Those machines need a different SMC key than older Intel models, and ChillMac only ever wrote the old one. Sliders moved and reported success while the fan stayed on automatic
- **Performance Mode no longer claims fans it cannot reach.** With the helper missing, the toggle, the curve percentage and the sliders all looked live. Monitoring was never affected: temperature, RPM, CPU, memory and battery readings have always been accurate
- **The privileged helper now verifies who is talking to it.** It previously accepted fan commands from any process on your Mac, and now accepts them only from ChillMac
- **Fans are reliably returned to automatic when you quit.** That request was sent as the app exited and usually did not arrive, leaving fans pinned at their last manual speed
- **Two crashes and a hang.** One in memory reporting, one in the FPS counter, and a stall that could freeze the app while it read hardware information

## Improvements

- **Smoother sliders and lighter background work.** Setting a fan speed takes a fraction of the work it used to, dragging a slider sends a couple of commands instead of one per step, and battery and disk readings moved off the main thread
