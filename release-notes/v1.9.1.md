## Highlights

Two things you can now switch on: the current temperature beside the menu bar icon, and keeping fans under ChillMac's control when you close the lid while plugged in. Both are off by default, so nothing changes unless you want it to.

## Added

- **Temperature in the menu bar.** Settings, under Display. "When Warm" leaves the menu bar alone until the Mac actually heats up and shows the reading only then, matching the Good / Warm / Hot wording in the main window. "Always" keeps it there the whole time. It follows your °F/°C setting, and stays plain text so it looks right on any wallpaper and in both light and dark menu bars
- **Keep Fans When Closed on Power.** Settings, under Fan Control. While on AC, fans stay exactly as ChillMac set them through closing the lid, locking the screen and sleeping, instead of being handed straight back to macOS. Useful for a docked Mac driving an external display. On battery nothing changes, so closing the lid away from a charger still returns the fans to automatic
