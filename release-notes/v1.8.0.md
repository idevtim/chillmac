## Highlights

Performance Mode gains a fifth level: **Ultra**. Unlike Max (smart preemptive balance) or a constant full-blast setting, Ultra is a pure-performance curve — higher floor, earlier ramp to 100% — so fans cool sooner and help hold peak clocks under load, without spinning at 100% when the machine is cool.

## New

- **Ultra performance level** — throttle-avoidance curve with a 70% floor, ramping to 100% by ~60°C peak zone temp (vs Max’s 50% floor and ~68°C). Faster ramp rates and lighter smoothing than Max so cooling reacts earlier when heat builds
- **Not Full Blast** — Ultra stays temperature-responsive. At cool temps it holds a high baseline (~70%), not constant 100%; Max remains the smart-balance preset

## Improvements

- Performance Mode segmented control now offers five levels: Low / Medium / High / Max / Ultra
- Mode description discloses that Ultra is louder earlier in exchange for sustained performance headroom
