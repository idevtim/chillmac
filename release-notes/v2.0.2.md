## Fixed

- **Fans waited for the menu bar to be opened.** At login or right after an update, ChillMac's fan helper can take a few seconds to start, and ChillMac only checked on it again when you opened the menu bar. Until then the fans stayed with macOS. ChillMac now keeps checking in the background and takes over as soon as the helper is ready.

Temperatures, the menu bar reading and the detail panels were accurate throughout. Only the start of Smart cooling was delayed.
